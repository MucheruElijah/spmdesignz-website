How to use this signature in Spacemail:

  1. Select and copy the signature box below (or click Copy Signature).
  2. Open your Spacemail Webmail.
  3. Go to Settings (Gear Icon) > Signatures (or Mail > Signature).
  4. Paste (Ctrl + V or Cmd + V) directly into the signature box.
  5. Click Save!
